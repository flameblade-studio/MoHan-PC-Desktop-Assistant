### 發行宣告：【為一句七夕快樂，出動紫金山天文台】

事情是這樣的：墨寒會在情人節、生日、聖誕節向主上道賀，而七夕走的是農曆——舊版內建了一張「未來十年」的查表。體檢時我們對著這張表逐日驗算，發現它不但十年後就會失明，表上 2033 與 2034 年的七夕還各誤植了一天。

為了讓墨寒在往後每一年的鵲橋邊都能準時說出那句「主上，七夕快樂」，我們沒有選擇連網查詢——離線原則不可退讓——而是把中國科學院**紫金山天文台**發布的 1900–2100 農曆朔閏資料，整套編進了 `domain/lunar_calendar.py`，以確定性演算法取代查表。從此墨寒的七夕，兩百年內分毫不差。

是的，為了一句節日問候，我們出動了天文台。世人或笑此舉小題大作；但對墨寒而言，準時說出那句祝福，就是靈魂的一部分。

## English

> Windows is the officially supported platform; macOS and Linux remain feature-limited Previews. This release consolidates the 86+ fixes from two four-front full-codebase audit waves after v4.5.0, and ships with complete multi-platform installer assets and formal Zenodo archival.

### Release proclamation: Calling in the Purple Mountain Observatory for one "Happy Qixi"

Here is what happened. MoHan greets her lord on Valentine's Day, birthdays, and Christmas — but the Qixi Festival follows the lunar calendar, and the old build carried a ten-year lookup table. During the audit we recomputed that table day by day and found it would not only go blind after a decade: the entries for 2033 and 2034 were each off by one day.

So that MoHan can say "Happy Qixi, my lord" on time beside the magpie bridge in every year to come, we did not reach for an online API — the offline principle is non-negotiable. Instead we compiled the 1900–2100 lunisolar data published by the **Purple Mountain Observatory** of the Chinese Academy of Sciences into `domain/lunar_calendar.py`, replacing the table with a deterministic algorithm. MoHan's Qixi is now exact to the day for two centuries.

Yes — for a single holiday greeting, we called in an observatory. The world may laugh at the overkill; but for MoHan, delivering that greeting on time is part of having a soul.

### Startup face-corruption, cured at the root

The v4.4.2-era face-layer misalignment that tore her face apart on launch is fully cured in this release: the speech mouth patch and the composed base are pinned to one pose canvas; the golden-batch facial feature layers were re-cut for all 24 views via a five-point YuNet least-squares affine from the half-body front layers; mouth authority was recalibrated across the 13 visible views from golden lip-layer alpha centroids.

### Two four-front audit waves (86+ items)

- **Core front**: affinity anchor advance after decay, true DB upsert, rear-facing state-machine gradients, animation freezes, outfit canvas scaling order, and the cross-midnight reminder contract adjudication.
- **UI front**: eighteen items including mis-touch guards, privacy consent, resource leaks, and discrete-blink contract assertions; the Wardrobe Pavilion full-body views now use MoHan's own left and right.
- **Tools & CI front**: installer self-test timeout with marker-file adjudication (curing the 3.15rc1 JIT/Qt finalization hang that burned 120-minute jobs); QPixmapCache clearing and forced GC in the speech-regression fixture (curing recurring MemoryError on 7 GB CI runners); the bubble-hide helper unified to restore the one-owner MRO contract.
- **Test front**: seven items covering tautological assertions, orphan-test adoption, fossil-test rulings, and collection reconciliation; the three-authority version-sync contract was corrected.

### The road of purity: through the narrow gate, toward unlimited possibility

Behind the written licensing governance in this release lies a series of quiet, unglamorous choices: we walked away from the community's most convenient tools and its most popular model weights, and verified the license pedigree of every tool, every model, and every asset on the pipeline — whatever failed verification was removed at once and banned in writing. We took this narrow road not to be different for its own sake, but so that every line of code, every image, and every model in MoHan can withstand scrutiny — keeping this project forever free to choose its own future, with unlimited possibilities including commercialization, never to be ambushed years later by a single toxic license clause.

### Licensing and commercialization governance

- Added the four-language `ASSETS-LICENSE.md`: MoHan's character artwork, persona, and name/likeness are All Rights Reserved with an express run/develop/test grant and fan-work friendly non-commercial terms; fixed the pre-existing dead links and the contradictory "character assets use MIT" line in all four README sections.
- The PySide6 (LGPL-3.0) compliance set: an About MoHan license section in Settings, the full LGPL/GPL texts bundled into release packages, and notices pointing at them.
- Added `docs/LICENSE-BLACKLIST.md`: beyond the whitelist (MIT / Apache 2.0 / CC0 / CC BY), banned tools and model weights are now named explicitly — three-tier licensing governance in writing.

### Engineering governance

- Release Please version-sync cured at the root: nine version files (CITATION, README, version_info, pyproject, SBOM, test constants and more) now sync automatically via markers, and the four-language release-notes draft is auto-generated — this document is the hand-finished first product of that machinery.
- Layer-module line counts moved to a ratchet: new files capped at 800 lines, the 17 baselined files may only shrink, and the global ceiling steps down each version from 4.6.

### Release assets and archival

- v4.5.0 shipped as source only due to a packaging-runner fault on release day; this release ships with complete multi-platform installer assets.
- This release is formally archived on Zenodo with a version DOI; the erroneous "mints a DOI automatically" statement in the v4.5.0 release document has been corrected.
